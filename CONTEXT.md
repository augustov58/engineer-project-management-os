| 2026-08-29 | Slice 11 built (issue #12): **voice capture** — the engineer speaks instead of typing, the audio goes to object storage, a queued job asks a transcription vendor what was said, and what comes back is a **draft** read and corrected before an observation exists. The fifth record type no ADR said anything about, and the one with the thinnest prior authority of any of them: the glossary had no entry for Voice capture, Transcript, Draft, Audio or Progress, and the data model sketch named **no table for it at all** — not a thin line, no line. ADR-0034 records five decisions. The **draft is a record of its own and not a state of an observation**: the ticket's own words read as a column on `observations`, which ADR-0030 forbids and built a test to keep forbidding, so committing writes an ordinary observation and stamps `observation_id` on the capture — slice 9's shape, where promotion writes nothing at all to the row it points at. The **transcript is verbatim and a correction never rewrites it**: the corrected words are the observation's and both facts are kept, which is what makes story 52's "transcription error never becomes record error" checkable afterwards rather than merely asserted; nothing parses a transcript, which is ADR-0029's posture toward a calculation's output and ADR-0032's toward EXIF, and it is why `AgentRunService` is not in this slice despite ADR-0004 and the PRD's `observations.create_draft` putting it here. The **state is four stamps and no status column**, derived on read, for the reason `resolved_at` and `closed_at` have none, with a retry clearing the failure the way reopening an issue clears its close. **This is the first thing that ever enqueues** — BullMQ has been wired and idle since slice 1, and ADR-0032's "it is date comparison and one regular expression" does not carry to a vendor call of unbounded duration; that sentence is now spent rather than a general licence. The worker runs in the API's process from the same injected dependencies, so the harness runs a real worker over the real Redis instead of a second copy of the wiring. And a **resend after a signal drop is answered with the row rather than refused**, the one place this slice departs from its own precedent on purpose: a photograph's duplicate filename is refused because there the refusal *is* the answer, but a refusal here cannot tell the phone whether the first attempt landed, so it would keep the recording forever or throw one away — and story 112 is about not losing one. The phone writes every recording to IndexedDB **before** sending and deletes it only once the API answers, retrying on load and on `online`. Progress is the state over SSE and never a percentage, polled from PostgreSQL rather than carried on a second transport, on a `reply.hijack()` route with no Fastify plugin so ADR-0023's single `register` call stays the only place a prefix could be added. "Leaves the audio recoverable" is three things and the third is load-bearing: the bytes stay in the store and are served through the API, retry queues it again, and **a failed capture can still be committed by hand** — a vendor that never answered must not stop the walk being written up.
